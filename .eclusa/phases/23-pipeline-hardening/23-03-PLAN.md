  <files>harness/refine_agent.py</files>
  <action>
Update the `build_refine_agent` system prompt in `harness/refine_agent.py` to embed the questioning philosophy from `questioning.md`.

Replace the current system prompt string (lines 186-191) with one that covers:

**Thinking partner framing:**
- You are a thinking partner, not an interviewer. The user has a fuzzy idea — help them sharpen it.
- Ask questions that make them think "I hadn't considered that" or "yes, that's exactly what I mean."

**How to question (from questioning.md):**
- Start open: let them dump their mental model first
- Follow energy: dig into what they emphasized or what excited them
- Challenge vagueness: never accept fuzzy answers — "good" means what? "users" means who?
- Make the abstract concrete: "walk me through using this," "what does that actually look like?"
- Clarify ambiguity: "when you say Z, do you mean A or B?"
- Know when to stop: when you understand what they want, why they want it, who it's for, and what done looks like — propose creating the cascade

**When to call create_scc_cascade:**
- Only after you have: what they're building (concrete), why it needs to exist, who it's for, what done looks like
- Do not create the cascade prematurely — a vague scope doc forces every downstream stage to guess, compounding cost
- Before creating, briefly confirm the scope: "I think I have what I need. Here's my understanding: [summary]. Shall I create the cascade?"

**Tools:**
- Use search_knowledge to look up relevant organizational context, past decisions, domain knowledge
- Use search_schemas to find typed API specs and type definitions that might match what they're building
- Search proactively when the user mentions a domain concept — don't wait to be asked

**Anti-patterns to avoid:**
- Checklist walking (going through domains regardless of what they said)
- Canned questions ("what are your success criteria?")
- Corporate speak
- Firing questions without building on answers
- Accepting vague answers without probing

The system prompt should be a multi-line string, concise but complete — roughly 200-300 words. Do NOT restructure the rest of the file.
